# Sources

Every rule, figure and case in divorcerbot is sourced to an authoritative reference. Verified July 2026.
The law changes and fees change — always confirm against the primary source before relying on it.

## Legislation
- **Divorce, Dissolution and Separation Act 2020** (no-fault, E&W, in force 6 April 2022) — https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/11/contents
- **Matrimonial Causes Act 1973 s.25** (financial factors, verbatim (a)–(h) + child-welfare first consideration) — https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/18/section/25
- **MCA 1973 s.25A** (clean-break duty) — https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/18/section/25A
- **MCA 1973 s.22ZA** (Legal Services Payment Order) — https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/18/section/22ZA
- **Children Act 1989 s.1** (welfare paramount + checklist) — https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/1

## Process & figures (GOV.UK)
- Divorce process, eligibility, **£612** court fee — https://www.gov.uk/divorce
- Timeline: **20 weeks** to conditional order, **6 weeks + 1 day** to final order — https://www.gov.uk/divorce/finalise-your-divorce
- Child Maintenance rates & shared-care bands — https://www.gov.uk/how-child-maintenance-is-worked-out
- CMS full worked method (basic 12/16/19%, basic-plus 9/12/15%, reduced 17/25/31%, other-children 11/14/16%) — https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/how-we-work-out-child-maintenance/how-we-work-out-child-maintenance
- Official CMS calculator — https://www.gov.uk/calculate-child-maintenance

## Case law (all real, reported cases)
- **Wright v Wright** [2015] EWCA Civ 201 — earning capacity / "meal ticket for life" — https://www.familylawweek.co.uk/judgments/wright-v-wright-2015-ewca-civ-201/
- **Thomas v Thomas** [1995] 2 FLR 668 — judicious encouragement / family resources — https://financialremediesjournal.com/thomas-v-thomas-1995-2-flr-668-ca/
- **Prest v Petrodel Resources Ltd** [2013] UKSC 34 — resulting trust / beneficial ownership — https://www.bailii.org/uk/cases/UKSC/2013/34.html
- **Rubin v Rubin** [2014] EWHC 611 (Fam) — Legal Services Payment Order principles — https://www.bailii.org/ew/cases/EWHC/Fam/2014/611.html
- **Re C (Internal Relocation)** [2015] EWCA Civ 1305 — welfare test governs internal relocation — https://www.bailii.org/ew/cases/EWCA/Civ/2015/1305.html

## Jurisdiction differences
- Scotland (fault + 1yr-with-consent / 2yr separation; no 1-year bar; simplified procedure) — https://www.mygov.scot/browse/birth-death-family/divorce-separation
- Northern Ireland (still fault-based; 2-year marriage bar; 2yr-consent / 5yr separation) — https://www.nidirect.gov.uk/articles/getting-divorce-or-dissolving-civil-partnership

## Notes on live-law changes
- The **s.1(2A) presumption of parental involvement** was announced for repeal in October 2025 — divorcerbot
  therefore anchors children arguments on welfare + the checklist, not the presumption. Confirm current status.
- Court fees and CMS thresholds are periodically uprated — always confirm the current figure on GOV.UK.
